# CodeQuity2025 – Build It. Pitch It. Get Backed.

## Overview
CodeQuity2025 is an online startup-oriented hackathon that transforms winning ideas into investor-ready ventures. Participants build a real MVP, pitch to real investors, and compete for funding opportunities, prizes, mentorship, and an on-chain NFT badge recognizing their contribution.

## Timeline & Mode
- **Mode:** Online
- **Event Dates:** 4 July 2025 – 16 July 2025

## Problem Statement / Themes
Build any **original tech product** that solves a real-world problem, impresses users, or challenges the status quo. Suggested domains include but are not limited to:
- FinTech & Crypto
- AI / ML Tools or Agents
- DevTools & SaaS
- Creator & E-commerce Tools
- HealthTech / EdTech & more
- Web3 / Blockchain Apps
- Open Innovation (anything bold, disruptive, and well-executed)

Projects **must** be built entirely within the hackathon timeframe.

## Required Deliverables
To qualify for judging and investor consideration, submissions **must** include:
1. **Project Title & Tagline** – A catchy, clear one-liner.
2. **Project Description** –
   - Problem being solved
   - Target users
   - How it works
   - Why it matters
3. **Tech Stack Used** – Languages, frameworks, APIs, tools.
4. **Public GitHub Repository** – Clean code, README, visuals.
5. **Demo or Walkthrough** – Live demo link (preferred) or short screen-recorded demo.
6. **Pitch Video (2–4 min)** – Explain product & business potential; close with a “why you should invest” message.
7. **Team Info** – Names, roles, and emails of all members.

_Submissions missing any of the above may be disqualified._

## Judging Criteria
1. **Innovation & Originality** – Novelty and disruptiveness of the idea.
2. **Technical Execution** – Code quality, scalability, functionality, effective tech use.
3. **Usefulness & Market Potential** – Real-world impact and viability as a product/startup.
4. **Design & User Experience** – UI cleanliness, intuitive flow, polish.
5. **Pitch Clarity & Investor Readiness** – Clear explanation of problem/solution and business promise.

## Prizes
- **Total Prize Pool:** $1,000
  - **Grand Prize:** $300 cash (1 winner)
  - **Tracks Prizes:** $100 cash (7 winners)
- **Additional Rewards:** Devpost achievements, mentorship, on-chain NFT badge, potential post-event funding.
